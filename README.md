## 🧠 Research Summary

I'm a **Graduate AI Researcher** at the **University of North Florida**, specialising in **Explainable AI (XAI) for clinical imaging**. I lead a multi-institutional XAI framework integrating **Grad-CAM, SHAP, LIME, and TCAV** across radiology and dermatology — validated by clinicians and tested for algorithmic fairness.

> *This work directly addresses the foundational research priorities in the 2018 NIH/RSNA/ACR AI in Medical Imaging Roadmap (Langlotz et al., 2019).*

Additional expertise in **multi-agent LLM systems**, **NLP-based information extraction**, and **production ML deployment on AWS**.

## 🔬 Featured Research Project

### 🩺 XAI Healthcare Diagnostics — Multi-Institutional Research (UNF, 2024–Present)

**Authors:** Arjun Vooturi, VenkatRam Reddy BaiReddy, Anjan Kumar Eruventi, Chenna Keshava Reddy Kodidala

[![View on GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-XAI--Healthcare--Diagnostics-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/arjunvooturi12/XAI-Artificial-Intelligence-in-Healthcare-Diagnostics)

**Datasets:** NIH ChestX-ray14 (112,120 X-rays, 14 diseases) + ISIC 2019 (25,331 skin images, 8 classes)

> **Novel Contribution:** First framework jointly integrating multi-method XAI + two imaging domains + clinician validation + fairness testing

- 🔍 Built a unified XAI framework diagnosing diseases from medical images and explaining predictions using **Grad-CAM** (spatial heatmaps), **SHAP** (feature attribution), **LIME** (local approximation), and **TCAV** (concept-based explanations)
- 🏥 Applied across **two imaging domains** — radiology (NIH ChestX-ray14, 14 pathologies) and dermatology (ISIC 2019, 8 classes) — demonstrating generalizability
- 👨‍⚕️ Conducted **clinician-centered validation**: AI heatmaps reviewed by medical professionals to assess alignment with clinical reasoning
- ⚖️ Implemented **fairness and algorithmic bias testing** across patient subgroups
